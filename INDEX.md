## 📊 Tech Stack Decision Matrix

| Aspek | Next.js ⭐ | WordPress | Laravel |
|-------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Setup Time** | 30 min | 1-2 hrs | 1 hr |
| **Learning Curve** | Medium | Easy | Hard |
| **Performance** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Scalability** | High | Medium | High |
| **Cost** | Low | Low | Low |
| **Customization** | Very High | Medium | Very High |
| **Maintenance** | Medium | Easy | Medium |
| **Best For** | Modern, Fast | CMS-Friendly | Enterprise |

### **🏆 REKOMENDASI: Next.js + Tailwind + Supabase**
- Modern & Fast
- SEO-optimized
- Enterprise-grade security
- Cost-effective
- Scalable untuk future
- Easy to maintain
